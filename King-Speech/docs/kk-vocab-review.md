# Kazakh synonym game — vocabulary review

File: constants/vocabularyDataKk.ts (300 words, ids / partOfSpeech / difficulty / timers unchanged, 33/33/34 per part of speech kept, 4–8 synonyms per word, helper w(...) untouched).

Reviewed 2026-11-20 against two online Kazakh synonym sources:

- **Ұлағат — «Синонимдер сөздігі»** (3 703 synonym rows of the printed Kazakh synonym dictionary): https://ulagat.com/category/sozdikter/sinonimder/
- **Sozdikqor.kz — Синонимайзер** of the Ш. Шаяхметов «Тіл-Қазына» national centre: https://sozdikqor.kz/sinonim

Method: every headword and every synonym was looked up in both sources (in both directions). A synonym counts as **attested** when a source puts it in the same synonym row as the headword. Synonyms that were not real synonyms (different meaning, hypernym, collocation, other part of speech) or not real words were removed; attested row members were added where the list was short. Some standard words are missing from both online sources (coverage is partial — e.g. loanwords, -лық/-лік abstract nouns, verb phrases); such clear, common synonyms were kept and are shown without a mark.

Totals: 300 entries; 245 changed (5 new headwords, 240 with edited synonym lists), 55 unchanged; 360 synonyms removed, 292 added. Of 1851 synonyms now in the file, 610 are attested in the sources (marked ✓ below); the rest are common usage, recommended for a native-speaker spot check.
